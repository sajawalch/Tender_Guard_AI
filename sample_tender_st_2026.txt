NOTICE INVITING TENDERS
Tender No: ST-2026-001
Organization: Capital Development Authority (CDA), Islamabad
Subject: Construction of Staff Residential Quarters (Type-C), Sector G-13, Islamabad

CDA invites sealed bids from PEC-registered constructors for the above work.
Estimated cost of the project is PKR 38,500,000. Bidders must hold a
valid PEC registration in category C-4 or higher. Bid security of
PKR 770,000 (2% of estimated cost) must accompany the bid in the form of
a call deposit or bank guarantee. The bidder must be on the FBR Active
Taxpayer List (ATL) at the time of bid submission; bids from non-ATL
firms will be rejected. An advance payment of 10% of the contract value
shall be released against a bank guarantee of equal amount. Interim
payments shall be made on a monthly basis upon certification of work
done. Liquidated damages for delay shall be charged at 0.067% of the
contract value per day, subject to a maximum of 10% of the contract
value. The bidder must demonstrate a minimum of 5 years of relevant
construction experience. Time for completion is 180 days from the date
of commencement.
