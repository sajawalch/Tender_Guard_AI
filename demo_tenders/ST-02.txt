**NOTICE INVITING TENDERS**
**Tender No:** ST-2026-002
**Organization:** National Highway Authority (NHA)
**Subject:** Rehabilitation of 12km Carriageway, N-25 Highway, Balochistan Section

NHA invites bids for rehabilitation works on the above road section.
Estimated cost: **PKR 420,000,000**. Bid security shall be **2.5% of the
bid value**. Bidders must possess **PEC category C-2 or above** with
specialization in road construction. The firm must be an active taxpayer
as per FBR's ATL. No advance payment shall be given for this contract.
Payments will be released on a **monthly** running-bill basis. Liquidated
damages are fixed at **0.1% per day** of contract value, capped at **10%**.
Bidders must have at least **10 years** of experience in highway/road
works. Completion period: **365 days**.
